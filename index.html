Morning HRV

Before you start
  1. Do it right after waking and using the toilet. Same time every day.
  2. Lie on your back and rest quietly for 1 minute.
  3. Cover the back camera and the flash with your index fingertip. Touch lightly, do not press.
  4. Keep your hand resting on your chest or the bed. Stay still and breathe normally.
